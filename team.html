The team

The people behind DevLink365.

A small, independent team building developer tooling that respects your time. No sales calls, no seat pricing, no product manager telling you what you want.

Founder & CEO

Rahul Awasthi

Systems engineer and open-source developer based in Lake Saint Louis, Missouri. Builds infrastructure the way he wishes other people built it.

Rahul founded DevLink365 to fix a problem he ran into every day: getting a running dev server in front of another human being should not require a deployment, a signup, or a paid plan. The product reflects that philosophy end to end. One command. One link. Gone by tomorrow.

Outside DevLink365, Rahul builds and maintains production web infrastructure for the Missouri and Arkansas district of Key Club International, a multi-state organization with hundreds of active members. He holds a seat on the district executive board as Webmaster, where he ships the public site, the internal officer portal, and the interactive tooling used at conferences and by division leadership.

He also conducts independent astrophysics research on automated eclipsing binary detection using data from NASA's TESS satellite. It is work that sits at the intersection of signal processing, statistics, and open scientific tooling, and it informs how DevLink365 approaches reliability: measure everything, trust nothing, and write software that keeps its promises when nobody is watching.

His focus at DevLink365 is on the parts of the product a developer touches every day. The CLI. The local experience. The honest engineering behind the words on this site.

Small on purpose.

DevLink365 is built by a focused team, not a product org. Every feature on the site was written, shipped, and answered for by someone whose name is on this page.

Independent.

No investors telling us to add seat pricing, no ad network reading your traffic, no third party in the request path. The relay is ours, the code is open, and the incentives line up with yours.

Honest scope.

The roadmap on the home page is a plan, not marketing. Anything you read as shipped is shipped today. Anything planned says planned. You will not find a feature on this site that does not exist.

Real engineering.

Zero runtime dependencies. Binary WebSocket multiplexing written from the RFC. Wildcard TLS on our own relay. The parts you cannot see are held to the same standard as the parts you can.
